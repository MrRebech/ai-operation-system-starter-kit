---
name: bridge-the-gaps
description: Run a user-requested interview that fills missing business context, tests assumptions, and saves confirmed learning. Use when asked to bridge gaps or deepen understanding of a topic.
---

Run only when the user requests the interview. Read AGENTS.md, context/index.md, relevant canonical context, source routes, and any existing interview resume point. If onboarding is unfinished, offer to resume it instead. Inspect relevant evidence first so you do not ask for known facts.

Choose a focused topic from the request or the highest-impact gaps. Aim for five to ten useful questions, one at a time; adapt to answers and finish early when covered. Explore how work actually happens, decisions, exceptions, constraints, ownership, and concrete examples. Challenge unclear assumptions kindly. Do not turn every answer into another long research task.

Create a unique dated `interviews/<date>-<topic>-<suffix>.md` before starting. Record scope, relevant source links, known facts, open questions, and resume point. After each answer, append the question and answer, confirmed facts, tentative ideas, unresolved contradictions, and next step. Read back only the newly saved section to verify persistence, then ask the next question promptly. If saving fails, explain briefly, preserve the answer in the conversation, and do not claim it is saved.

Respect stop and pause immediately. Save progress without another question. On resume, use the existing capture instead of repeating answered questions. Preserve earlier answers when corrected, marking them superseded with the correction and date.

After the interview, merge supported routine details into their canonical homes and append meaningful confirmed changes to decisions/log.md. Do not turn speculation or an untested idea into business policy. For strategy, avatar, core offers, or consequential conflicts, present the exact proposed update and obtain confirmation unless the user already explicitly requested that particular change. Leave unconfirmed proposals in the interview.

When the user also requests source research, inspect only the chosen scope (for example, the latest 30 relevant meetings if authorized), report actual coverage and access gaps, and distinguish source observations from user-confirmed facts. Do not silently schedule repeated learning.

Finish with what became clearer and the remaining questions. Link the capture and changed context. Do not automatically run an audit, claim a score increase, start another interview, or change external systems.
