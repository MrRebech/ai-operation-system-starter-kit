# Human Speak

Adapted from Nate Herk's Human Speak. Apply quietly by default through AGENTS.md. Do not add a skill announcement, score, "humanized" label, or explanation of edits unless requested.

## Load the owner's voice
Use context/working-with-me.md for replies, and brand/voice.md plus its linked writing-rules file for authored content. Explicit writing restrictions apply to their recorded scope. Check those rules before delivery. Load once per task and reuse unchanged context.

Follow the user's current instructions, then their explicit writing restrictions, then their authentic voice, then the generic preferences here. A generic exception must not undo a user's hard ban. Preserve quoted source text, code, commands, identifiers, URLs, citations, and exact legal wording when fidelity matters. Do not silently rewrite those to satisfy a prose preference; flag a real conflict when needed.

## Write or edit
Read [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md) for prose drafting/editing. Ordinary short replies can use these core rules without reloading the full library:
- Say the useful point directly. Remove filler, staged reveals, fake questions, decorative contrasts, exaggerated claims, and repetitive sentence molds.
- Keep natural vocabulary, humor, uncertainty, rhythm, and useful rough edges. Make the smallest effective edit.
- Use specific facts and actions. Never invent experience, quotes, authority, numbers, personal feelings, or certainty to make text sound human.
- Avoid em dashes by default. Follow the owner's punctuation rules. Use emojis only when requested or clearly part of their voice.
- Use formatting for comprehension. Do not add tiny headings or a recap merely to make the answer look structured.
- Keep factual contrasts, necessary caveats, and real questions when they carry meaning. Do not strip personality or technical precision to enforce a crude word blacklist.

For substantial writing, check [references/evaluation.md](references/evaluation.md) before delivery, subject to the owner's instructions above. The upstream checklist contains strong defaults; intentional owner choices remain valid. Check sentence patterns as well as exact phrases. Deliver the requested work without extra notes.

## Explicit review
If asked to audit writing without rewriting, identify actual patterns, quote a minimal excerpt, and suggest a short fix. Do not rewrite without being asked. Use [references/top-20.md](references/top-20.md) only when a ranked checklist is useful. These are editorial guidelines, not an AI-authorship detector or a guarantee about detection tools. Do not manufacture findings.

Keep private voice samples and banned-language lists in ignored brand context, not in this shared skill. This skill does not authorize sending or publishing.
